ASC Living Archives: shared data

This is the one source of material for every ASC archive interface, kept by the ASC Archives Working Group. Interfaces such as the Living Archive and Archive Lenses read from it, so a correction made here reaches all of them.

Everything has a permanent handle: item:…, person:…, place:…, concept:… and collection:….

  • data.json: all items, people, places, concepts and collections
  • neo4j/nodes.csv, neo4j/edges.csv and neo4j/load.cypher: the same material, ready to load into Neo4j

Originals remain with their authors and on asc-cybernetics.org. Links to anything containing personal contact details are withheld until reviewed.
